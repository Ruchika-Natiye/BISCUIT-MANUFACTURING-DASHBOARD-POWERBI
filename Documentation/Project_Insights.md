# Biscuit Manufacturing Dashboard – Business Insights & Analysis

## 1. Project Overview
<p>
The Biscuit Manufacturing Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides a comprehensive analysis of biscuit manufacturing operations, sales performance, production metrics, product contribution, regional performance, and distribution channels.
The dashboard transforms manufacturing and sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, analytical charts, and AI-powered analysis.

The primary objective is to help business stakeholders understand performance, identify opportunities, monitor operational efficiency, and make data-driven decisions. 
</p>

# 2. Project Objectives

1. Analyze overall biscuit sales and manufacturing performance.

2. Identify the highest-performing biscuit products.

3. Evaluate regional sales performance.

4. Compare sales across different distribution channels.

5. Analyze monthly sales trends and fluctuations.

6. Monitor production, units sold, and defect rate.

7. Identify factors influencing production using Power BI Key Influencers.

8. Generate actionable business recommendations.

# 4. Strategic Business Recommendations

## Sales Optimization

Focus on high-performing products and identify opportunities to improve sales of lower-performing products.

## Regional Expansion

Investigate the East region's comparatively lower sales and develop targeted marketing strategies.

## Distribution Management

Strengthen distributor relationships while evaluating the performance of other sales channels.

## Production Planning

Use historical sales trends to align production schedules with customer demand.

## Cost Optimization

Monitor labor costs and manufacturing output together to identify operational improvement opportunities.

## Quality Management

Validate the defect-rate calculation and establish reliable quality-control KPIs.

## Data-Driven Decision Making

Use Power BI dashboards and AI-powered analytical features to support continuous business monitoring.

# 5. Conclusion

The Biscuit Manufacturing Dashboard demonstrates how Microsoft Power BI can transform manufacturing and sales data into actionable business intelligence.

By combining KPI monitoring, product analysis, regional sales comparisons, channel performance, monthly trends, and AI-powered Key Influencers, the dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance.

The analysis helps identify leading products, understand regional differences, monitor sales fluctuations, and explore factors associated with production performance.

Overall, this project demonstrates practical applications of Business Intelligence, Data Analytics, DAX, and data visualization in the biscuit manufacturing industry.
